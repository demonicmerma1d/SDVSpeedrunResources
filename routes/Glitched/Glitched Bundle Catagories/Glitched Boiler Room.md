# Route
## Notes
- Version 1.6
- Chinese
- Female
- Standard bundles.

## Overview
The goal of this run is to complete the Boiler Room(autosplit available), which is unlocked after 2 bundles inside the Community Center. Time starts on pressing ok for a fresh file, and ends on the white flash of room completion. 

### File Creation and Introduction
Name the player the text in the paste.
Watch the introduction until Robin starts saying the name(press ESC 2x to skip the start), then click the skip cutscene button. With the current paste the cutscene should [crash](/glitches/Miscellaneous%20Glitches) and autoend.

### Day 1
1) Drink the triple shot expresso you have been given(this is faster than having a 2nd speed buff).
2) Place a Junimo Chest down, and put the 4 geologist bundle donation items into it. 
3) Go outside, read the mail and run to the Community Center, heading to the craft room. Place the junimo chest down in the middle of the Community Center on the way past.
6) At the crafts room, turn in the summer foraging and construction bundles. 
7) [Animation Cancel](/Animation%20Cancelling.md) to regain control, place the junimo chest and take out the 4 items, and run to the Boiler Room location, ACing as required to maintain control.
8) When the Boiler Room opens, donate the remaining items to finish the room.
