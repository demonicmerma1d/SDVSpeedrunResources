# Route
## Notes
- Version 1.6
- Chinese
- Female
- Standard bundles.

## Overview
The goal of this run is to complete the Vault(autosplit available), which is unlocked after 2 bundles inside the Community Center. Time starts on pressing ok for a fresh file, and ends on the white flash of room completion. 

### File Creation and Introduction
Name the player the text in the paste.
Watch the introduction until Robin starts saying the name(press ESC 2x to skip the start), then click the skip cutscene button. With the current paste the cutscene should [crash](/glitches/Miscellaneous%20Glitches) and autoend.

### Day 1
1) Drink the triple shot expresso you have been given(this is faster than having a 2nd speed buff).
2) Go outside, read the mail and run to the Community Center, heading to the Crafts room.
3) At the crafts room, turn in the summer foraging bundle, close the menu to start the animations,[Animation Cancel](/Animation%20Cancelling.md) to regain control, and turn in the construction bundle. 
4) AC to regain control and run to the fishtank location, ACing as required throughout to maintain control. When the fishtank appears, turn in the night fish bundle.
5) Run to the Bulliten Board location and turn in fodder bundle, you do not need to wait for the plague to appear, then go to the vault.
6) Wait for the vault plague to appear, buy all the bundles.

Credit to lpandak for the easier movement bundle choices. (Time equivelently you could use summer foraging quality crops, spring crops and summer crops, but that would involve more blind movement potentially)
