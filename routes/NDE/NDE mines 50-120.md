# Route
### Important Notes
- Chinese
- Version 1.6

### File Setup
Player:
`%itemid 70 999%%`

Farm:
`[261][689]%favorite`

Favorite:
`[253][(H)32][896][]`

### Run notes:
Time starts on pressing ok for a fresh file, and ends on reaching floor 50/120(autosplit available). The goal is reach floor 50/120 of the mines.
### Start
- Any farm.
- Watch introduction until farm name is spoken(the cutscene will auto crash and end).
- Day 1, move the bed, sell the galaxy soul in the shipping bin, go back to bed(hold click to speed up the shipping menu, and then reclick the ok button).(Optimal sleeping in chinese you should be clicking with a mouse on the sleep yes/no prompt, it saves about 0.5s per sleep)
- Sleep until day 7.
- On day 7, drink the triple shot expresso and go outside and read the mail once.
- Warp to the desert(desert warp totem) with your 999 jades, and trade them at the desert trader for 999 staircases(on keyboard you can press ctrl+shift+1 while clicking to buy a stack at once).
- Warp to the mines(mountain warp totem) and ladder the mines until 50/120. It is recommended to simply run mines 120 and have a split for mines 50 so you can just do both at once.(The hat is for swag if you want to wear it)
